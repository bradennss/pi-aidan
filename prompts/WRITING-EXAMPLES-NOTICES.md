# Writing example notices

The examples in `system.md` are verbatim excerpts, ordered from shorter passages to longer ones. Markdown block quotes, whitespace, and link presentation are formatting; linked words are retained without their link destinations. The rest of the prompt is unchanged by this example selection.

## Reader recommendations

Candidates were found through reader recommendations and discussions, then checked against the original writing. These discussions explain why the sources were selected; they are not part of the injected prompt.

- **Julia Evans:** [Ask HN: Examples of good technical writing?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=31630915). Readers describe her writing as “fun and sympathetic about how hard all this junk is” and her work as “Accessible, educational and full of personality.” The discussion specifically recommends the DNS zine announcement used below.
- **Josh W. Comeau:** [Ask HN: Favorite Blogs by Individuals?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=27302195) recommends his blog. [Ask HN: What are some blog posts that you have enjoyed going through?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=33715396) says his articles are “very good at explaining things” and specifically recommends the Flexbox guide.
- **Amos / fasterthanli.me:** [Ask HN: Favorite Blogs by Individuals?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=27302195). Readers praise the articles as “long, informative, and fun” and the author's ability to make material “accessible” and “interesting.” “What's in the box?” is among the recommended posts.
- **Joel Spolsky:** [Ask HN: What are your favourite developer blogs?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=15154903) recommends Joel on Software. [Ask HN: Examples of good technical writing?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=31630915) calls Spolsky “excellent,” and [How to impress Joel Spolsky](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=1994193) discusses his enjoyable prose and narrative approach.
- **The Rust Programming Language:** [Learning Rust via Advent of Code](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=19077354). A reader calls the book “the best technical writing I've read in memory.”
- **Django documentation:** [Ask HN: What's the best documentation you've ever read?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=17399340). A reader specifically praises the documentation as “well written, approachable,” alongside its layered structure and useful caveats.
- **Ember Guides:** [Ember.js Basics — What is Ember?](https://www.infragistics.com/community/blogs/b/brent_schooley/posts/ember-js-basics-what-is-ember). This developer's introduction calls the guides “very well-written.”
- **Effective Go:** [Effective Go discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=28735620) praises its balanced advice and avoidance of empty “best practice” language. [Another discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=31167935) praises its lack of fluff.
